# Design Notes — Existing Site (corrected from real screenshots)

> Captured from owner-provided screenshots of all 3 pages, 2026-05-31.

## Visual identity (current Google Site)

- **Logo:** circular badge — volleyball + green motif, top-left on every page.
- **Colours:**
  - Navy blue `~#1f3a5f` — all headings ("NORTHSTOWE", page titles).
  - Sage / mint green `~#7cbf9e` — section sub-headings, banner backgrounds,
    nav underline.
  - Cream / off-white `~#f3f1e7` — page background.
- **Hero illustration:** flat-style art of hands reaching for a volleyball over a
  net with clouds — friendly, approachable, not corporate.
- **Typography:** mix — a heavy condensed display for "NORTHSTOWE", lighter serif-
  ish for "Social Volleyball Club", clean sans for body.
- **Tone:** warm, inclusive, community-first. "Social" is the whole point.

## Implication for the new site

My first build used the WRONG anchor (forest green `#3A5A40` from a misread
capture) and a "Bump/Set/Spike" competitive hero. That's off-brand for a
**social, pre-launch, all-abilities** club. Correct direction:

- Anchor on **navy + sage-green + cream** (carry over real brand).
- Lead with **community / welcome / "there's a spot for you"**, NOT competition.
- Keep energy/modern polish to attract young people, but frame around *belonging*
  and *getting started*, not leagues (league play is a future aspiration only).
- Surface the real pillars: Purpose, Our Goal, Our Story, What We Offer, Meet the
  Coach, and the "register your interest" form (since there are no sessions yet).
